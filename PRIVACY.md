# raidDRAFTpro Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 26 September 2026

raidDRAFTpro is a Mac word processor in the Raid Bay product suite. It runs on your computer.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not create an account, does not include analytics, does not include advertising, and does not send your documents to a server.

There are no third parties. No SDKs phone home.

## What stays on your Mac

- Documents you create, open, or export
- Comments attached to those documents
- Preferences such as theme, focus width, spelling, and whether you have seen the start screen

These are stored in files you choose and in standard macOS app preferences (UserDefaults).

## Retention and deletion

You control the files. Delete a document in Finder to delete it. Uninstalling raidDRAFTpro removes the app and its preferences. There is no account to delete and no server copy to request.

## Permissions

raidDRAFTpro uses the App Sandbox. It reads and writes only files you pick in Open/Save panels, and it can print.

The app does not require network access.

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the updated policy will be posted at the same URL.
